CYD Miner Kit
=============

This installer provides the Njörðr seas CYD miner app and ESP32-2432S028 firmware
for USB Bitcoin SHA-256 mining. Pool traffic runs on your PC; the board
only hashes work received over USB-C.

No warranty. Solo Bitcoin mining on ESP32 is educational / lottery odds.
Use at your own risk. You are responsible for pool credentials and funds.
